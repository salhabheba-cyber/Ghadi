# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ghadi's community to be welcoming, harassment-free and constructive for everyone, regardless of age, background, ability, identity, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or beliefs.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for learners and the project.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or trolling.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people who violate this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example by using a repository security advisory or the contact details on a maintainer's profile). Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.

This document is a short, original summary in the spirit of widely used community codes of conduct.
